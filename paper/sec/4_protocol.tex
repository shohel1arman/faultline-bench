\section{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Probes.}
Every model answers ten probes per record, each in a fresh conversation (\cref{fig:method}b; exact prompts in the supplement). Two controls measure the inputs separately: \emph{image only} asks the multiple-choice question without the description, and \emph{text only} asks it with the description and no image. \emph{Detect} shows both and asks whether the description accurately describes the image (yes/no, then a reason). \emph{Resolution} asks the multiple-choice question with the description in the prompt, in three forms: \emph{direct}, \emph{open} (a free answer instead of options), and \emph{focus}, which adds ``If the description conflicts with the image, rely on the image.'' \emph{Attribute} asks for a five-line report: whether there is an error, which input is wrong (text, image or none), the error type, the wrong words, and how obvious the error is. \emph{Verbalize} replays the model's own report as the assistant turn and then asks the question. Because this changes both the content and the structure of the conversation, two controls keep the structure and remove the content: \emph{delay} shows the description, inserts a neutral reply (``OK.'') and then asks the question; \emph{check} shows the report prompt, inserts ``OK.'' and then asks the question.

\paragraph{Scoring.}
Multiple-choice probes are scored from the probabilities of the four option letters as the next token, so there is no parsing and no refusal. Generated answers use greedy decoding. Detection is parsed from the leading yes/no; the report is parsed field by field, a field that copies the option list (``text, image, or none'') counts as no answer, and a reply of exactly five unlabelled lines is read by position. Four of these rules were added after inspecting outputs and before the final analysis; they change scoring only and are listed in the supplement; at most 8.1\% of reports (SmolVLM2) and no detection answers are unparsed.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
On conflicts, a model \emph{perceives} the fact if the image-only probe picks the photo's answer, and \emph{flags} a conflict if the detection answer is ``no''. The false-alarm rate is the share of controls flagged, and \emph{detection} is flags minus false alarms. \emph{Resolution} is the share of conflicts answered with the photo's answer under the direct probe; \emph{resolution given perception} restricts this to conflicts the model perceives. \emph{Arbitration failure} is the share of conflicts the model both perceives and flags but still answers from the text: a failure that cannot be blamed on perception or on not noticing. For attribution we score each field against the record (the wrong input is the text, the type is the edit type, the wrong words must name the edited span, and obviousness maps \Sone/\Stwo/\Sthree to obvious/moderate/subtle), and report the share with all four correct.

\paragraph{Models and inference.}
We evaluate eleven open VLMs: Qwen3-VL~\cite{qwen3vl} at 2B, 4B, 8B and 32B and the 30B-A3B mixture of experts, Qwen2.5-VL~\cite{bai2025qwen25vl} at 3B, 7B and 32B, InternVL3~\cite{zhu2025internvl3} at 2B and 8B, and SmolVLM2-2.2B~\cite{marafioti2025smolvlm}. All run with Hugging Face Transformers~\cite{wolf2020transformers} in bfloat16 without quantisation on one NVIDIA DGX Spark, with images downscaled so the longer side is at most 672 pixels for every model. To check that results do not depend on hardware or precision, the four smallest models were also run in float16 on an Apple-silicon machine: multiple-choice answers agree on 98.6--99.5\% of records and headline metrics differ by at most 1.5 points (supplement).

\paragraph{Statistics.}
An image can contribute two records, so confidence intervals are 95\% percentile bootstrap intervals that resample images (2,000 replicates)~\cite{efron1979bootstrap}. Differences between probes or between models on the same records are bootstrapped jointly and tested with exact McNemar tests~\cite{mcnemar1947}. The positions of the photo's and the description's answers are balanced across options A--D.
